Privacy policy

As of 4 November 2026

1. Who is responsible?

Tourchef is a booking tool for mountain guides. It is operated by {{ operator_name }}, {{ operator_address }} ("Tourchef", "we").

  • Guest data (requests, sign-ups, payments): the mountain guide you book with is responsible. Tourchef processes this data on their behalf (as a processor).
  • Guide data and the operation of this website: Tourchef is responsible.

Privacy contact: {{ privacy_email }}

2. What data we process

Guests: name, email, phone, number of participants or seats, tour wish and messages, chosen dates, language, when you accepted the terms, payment and refund status. For sign-ups to scheduled dates also: an emergency contact and a short note on experience and level. Card details only go to our payment provider Stripe, not to Tourchef and not to the guide.

Guides: login data (email, password as a hash, encrypted two-factor key), name, phone, day rates, terms, IBAN, VAT number, availability, the address of an external calendar and the busy days taken from it, Stripe account ID.

Technical: IP address (briefly, to prevent abuse; for guides in the access log), server logs of our host (time, address requested, IP).

3. Why

  • To handle requests, bookings and sign-ups and to keep you informed by email
  • Payments, invoices, receipts and refunds
  • Safety on tours: the emergency contact and your experience help the guide plan the tour and act in an emergency
  • Secure operation (abuse prevention, troubleshooting) and legal obligations (bookkeeping)

For guests from the EU, processing is based on performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), legal obligations (c) and our legitimate interest in secure operation (f).

4. Who receives the data

  • The guide you book with. Other guides do not see your data.
  • Google Cloud (running the application, Zurich region, Switzerland)
  • Neon (database, Frankfurt region, EU)
  • Stripe Payments Europe Ltd. (Ireland) and Stripe, Inc. (USA) for payments and refunds
  • Our email delivery service [enter provider]

Data is only transferred to the USA with appropriate safeguards (EU/Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

5. How long

  • Booking, invoice and payment data: 10 years (statutory retention)
  • Phone, emergency contact and experience: without a fixed limit, so the guide can lead you safely on further tours without you having to enter everything again. We delete them at any time on request (see section 7); bookings and invoices are kept.
  • Waiting lists: until the tour date
  • Server logs: usually 30 days

Deleted data also disappears from backups once the backup period has passed.

6. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Phone, emergency contact and experience are additionally encrypted in the database; the key is kept separately from it. Guides log in with a password and a second factor; access to emergency contacts and exports are logged. Your booking page can only be reached through a secret link.

7. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, have it corrected or deleted, and ask for a copy. Contact the guide you booked with or {{ privacy_email }}. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) and, as a guest from the EU, to your supervisory authority.

8. Cookies

We only use technically necessary cookies: a session cookie for logged-in guides and a cookie for the chosen language. No tracking, no advertising, no analytics.

9. Changes

We update this policy when Tourchef changes. The version published here applies.
